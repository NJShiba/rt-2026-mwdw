Why taxes are a line here: most of your withdrawals (401k/IRA, gains in brokerage) are taxed — to spend $100 you may need to withdraw $120+. The effective rate depends heavily on which accounts your $4.7M sits in (traditional vs. Roth vs. taxable); 15–25% is a common range. Social Security is treated as untaxed here for simplicity (up to 85% can be taxable — adjust the rate up slightly to compensate). A tax-aware withdrawal strategy is worth a session with a CPA or planner.
