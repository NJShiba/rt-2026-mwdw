Confidence in hitting the goal

Confidence comes from 1500 simulated market histories: instead of one smooth return every year, each run draws a random yearly return around your expected average, so good and bad decades land in different orders. The percentage is the share of runs that finish at or above your legacy target without running dry first — this is what captures sequence-of-returns risk, the danger a straight-line projection hides.
